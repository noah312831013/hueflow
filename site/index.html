Questions.

How does the free trial work?

Download and use everything for 7 days — no account, no card. When the trial ends, the gradients stop until you enter a license key.

How do I unlock it after buying?

Your license key arrives by email right after checkout. Click the trial badge in Hueflow, paste the key, and you're done.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes, one purchase covers up to 3 Macs you own. You can move a license by deactivating it in the app.

Will it drain my battery?

Hueflow pauses on battery power and behind full-screen apps by default, and whenever the screen saver is running. You can also pause it from the menu bar.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
